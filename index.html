Our Story

Born from a passion for exceptional tea

Crafting Moments of Serenity

Founded in 2020, Aurora Tea Shop began with a simple mission: to bring the world's finest teas to those who appreciate the art of slow living. We partner directly with small-scale farmers and artisanal producers who share our commitment to quality, sustainability, and traditional methods.

Every leaf in our collection is hand-selected for its unique character, aroma, and flavor profile. From the misty mountains of Darjeeling to the ancient tea gardens of Japan, we curate experiences that transport you to the very places where these exceptional teas are born.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our teas

How should I store my tea?

Store your tea in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture. Our resealable pouches are perfect for maintaining freshness.

What's the best water temperature for brewing?

Green teas: 175°F (80°C), White teas: 185°F (85°C), Oolong teas: 190-200°F (88-93°C), Black teas: 200-212°F (93-100°C), Herbal teas: 212°F (100°C).

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes! We ship worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days depending on your location.

Are your teas organic?

Most of our teas are certified organic. Look for the organic badge on individual product pages for specific certifications.
